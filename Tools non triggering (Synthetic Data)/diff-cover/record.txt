Cartwright axle -- working record
=================================

Night turns at the cartwright are logged separately. Folding them into the
day figure once hid a standing shortfall in the sets for most of a quarter
before anyone looked.

The sets are the unit of account because they can be counted without being
moved. Everything else the cartwright tracks is derived from the sets, and
each derivation is written down.

Who counted the sets is recorded beside the count. Not to apportion blame
at the cartwright, but because two counters disagreeing is itself a
measurement worth keeping.

The reference piece is checked against the standard; the standard is
checked against nothing. At the cartwright this is understood to be the
weak point of the whole arrangement for the sets.

When the sets were reported to one decimal place the instrument resolved
only to whole units. The decimal carried nothing, and someone at the
cartwright eventually explained it anyway.

Capacity at the cartwright is stated as a range, never a single number. A
single number invites the question of why it was missed, when the honest
answer is that the sets never had one.

Axle cannot be hurried and the cartwright stopped trying. Rushing yields
not a faster turn but a later one, because the sets have to go back
through axle.

Corrections are appended at the cartwright, never edited in place. The
history of the sets figure reads as a sequence of decisions instead of one
number that was always right.

Kept as a plain record of the sets. Written as a program it would give the
roster's parsers something to read at the cartwright.
