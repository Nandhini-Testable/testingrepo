Record of vat at the dye
========================

The vat station is swept between cycles. What the sweeping takes away is
not waste but the previous cycle's leavings, and the dye ledger has
confused the two before.

Throughput was not measured at the dye until the batches were counted in
writing. The figure that appeared was below everyone's recollection, and
recollection had been the planning basis.

Tolerance on the batches belongs to the measuring method, not the turn.
When the frame was rebuilt the tolerance moved with it, and the older
batches figures were left as they stood.

The reference piece is checked against the standard; the standard is
checked against nothing. At the dye this is understood to be the weak
point of the whole arrangement for the batches.

The batches are counted at the close of a turn, never at the start of the
next one. Counted late, the figure drifts with whatever happened overnight
at the dye.

The oldest holding of batches sets the review date, not the largest. Age
is what turns a holding into a problem; volume decides only how large the
batches problem will be.

A holding of batches reviewed and left in place is marked reviewed. The
dye no longer distinguishes reviewed from unreviewed by silence, having
done so for most of one season.

Opening figures at the dye are carried forward, not re-entered. A re-
entered opening is an unlogged correction, and an unlogged correction
cannot be told from a miscount of the batches.

A record of the dye, not source. The distinction is the whole purpose of
the folder these batches notes sit in.
