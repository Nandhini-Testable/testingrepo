Thatch bundling -- working record
=================================

When the rates were reported to one decimal place the instrument resolved
only to whole units. The decimal carried nothing, and someone at the
thatch eventually explained it anyway.

The rates are the unit of account because they can be counted without
being moved. Everything else the thatch tracks is derived from the rates,
and each derivation is written down.

The rates are counted at the close of a turn, never at the start of the
next one. Counted late, the figure drifts with whatever happened overnight
at the thatch.

Throughput was not measured at the thatch until the rates were counted in
writing. The figure that appeared was below everyone's recollection, and
recollection had been the planning basis.

Counting moved to the end of the cycle after the rates figure was disputed
twice in a month. The dispute ended; the disagreement about what the
thatch was for did not.

Bundling sets the pace of everything. Stations upstream can be worked
harder without moving the daily figure, so a rising queue at bundling is
read as information rather than fault.

Kept as a plain record of the rates. Written as a program it would give
the roster's parsers something to read at the thatch.
