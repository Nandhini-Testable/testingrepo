Cartwright axle -- working record
=================================

The queue at axle runs oldest first and exceptions are logged. The
exception log at the cartwright is reviewed before the exceptions are: a
rule broken often is a rule written wrong.

Conditions at axle are logged at the start and the end of a turn, never
averaged across the middle. An average hides a cartwright drifting
steadily in one direction.

Axle cannot be hurried and the cartwright stopped trying. Rushing yields
not a faster turn but a later one, because the sets have to go back
through axle.

Who counted the sets is recorded beside the count. Not to apportion blame
at the cartwright, but because two counters disagreeing is itself a
measurement worth keeping.

A holding of sets reviewed and left in place is marked reviewed. The
cartwright no longer distinguishes reviewed from unreviewed by silence,
having done so for most of one season.

Nothing in the cartwright record is an estimate. Where sets could not be
reached safely the location is omitted and the omission noted, rather than
filled with a guess.

Nothing in the cartwright file executes and nothing declares a project.
For the sets that is the only property here that matters.
