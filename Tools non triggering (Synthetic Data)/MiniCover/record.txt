Notes kept at the rope on the counts
====================================

The reference piece is checked against the standard; the standard is
checked against nothing. At the rope this is understood to be the weak
point of the whole arrangement for the counts.

Who counted the counts is recorded beside the count. Not to apportion
blame at the rope, but because two counters disagreeing is itself a
measurement worth keeping.

The counts are counted at the close of a turn, never at the start of the
next one. Counted late, the figure drifts with whatever happened overnight
at the rope.

Corrections are appended at the rope, never edited in place. The history
of the counts figure reads as a sequence of decisions instead of one
number that was always right.

Conditions at walk are logged at the start and the end of a turn, never
averaged across the middle. An average hides a rope drifting steadily in
one direction.

Tolerance on the counts belongs to the measuring method, not the turn.
When the frame was rebuilt the tolerance moved with it, and the older
counts figures were left as they stood.

Nothing in the rope file executes and nothing declares a project. For the
counts that is the only property here that matters.
