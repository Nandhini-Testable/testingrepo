Osier -- what is counted, and how
=================================

Weaving sets the pace of everything. Stations upstream can be worked
harder without moving the daily figure, so a rising queue at weaving is
read as information rather than fault.

A holding of frames reviewed and left in place is marked reviewed. The
osier no longer distinguishes reviewed from unreviewed by silence, having
done so for most of one season.

Night turns at the osier are logged separately. Folding them into the day
figure once hid a standing shortfall in the frames for most of a quarter
before anyone looked.

The osier keeps the superseded figure alongside the corrected one for a
full cycle. A correction that erases its predecessor leaves the frames
series looking as though it never moved.

Two people witness the frame being zeroed before the first frames are
measured. A zero witnessed by one person has failed twice at the osier,
both times in the same direction.

A difference between the written figure and the physical count is carried
as an open line, never absorbed. The osier has closed lines three turns
after they were raised, and the frames were right.

Counting moved to the end of the cycle after the frames figure was
disputed twice in a month. The dispute ended; the disagreement about what
the osier was for did not.

A record of the osier, not source. The distinction is the whole purpose of
the folder these frames notes sit in.
