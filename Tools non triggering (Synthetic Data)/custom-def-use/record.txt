Notes kept at the bell on the moulds
====================================

Sequence matters more than date in the bell record. Each change to how the
moulds were handled made the next one legible, and none would have been
noticed under the earlier practice.

A holding of moulds reviewed and left in place is marked reviewed. The
bell no longer distinguishes reviewed from unreviewed by silence, having
done so for most of one season.

The oldest holding of moulds sets the review date, not the largest. Age is
what turns a holding into a problem; volume decides only how large the
moulds problem will be.

The founding station is swept between cycles. What the sweeping takes away
is not waste but the previous cycle's leavings, and the bell ledger has
confused the two before.

The reference piece is checked against the standard; the standard is
checked against nothing. At the bell this is understood to be the weak
point of the whole arrangement for the moulds.

Corrections are appended at the bell, never edited in place. The history
of the moulds figure reads as a sequence of decisions instead of one
number that was always right.

Counting moved to the end of the cycle after the moulds figure was
disputed twice in a month. The dispute ended; the disagreement about what
the bell was for did not.

Nothing in the bell file executes and nothing declares a project. For the
moulds that is the only property here that matters.
