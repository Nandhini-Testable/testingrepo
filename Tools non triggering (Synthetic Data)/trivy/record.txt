Record of burning at the lime
=============================

Counting moved to the end of the cycle after the draws figure was disputed
twice in a month. The dispute ended; the disagreement about what the lime
was for did not.

When the draws were reported to one decimal place the instrument resolved
only to whole units. The decimal carried nothing, and someone at the lime
eventually explained it anyway.

Burning sets the pace of everything. Stations upstream can be worked
harder without moving the daily figure, so a rising queue at burning is
read as information rather than fault.

Loss between receipt and the close of burning is attributed, not written
off. The incoming lot is weighed before anything is done to it, which is
the one step the lime never skips.

Opening figures at the lime are carried forward, not re-entered. A re-
entered opening is an unlogged correction, and an unlogged correction
cannot be told from a miscount of the draws.

Sequence matters more than date in the lime record. Each change to how the
draws were handled made the next one legible, and none would have been
noticed under the earlier practice.

The burning station is swept between cycles. What the sweeping takes away
is not waste but the previous cycle's leavings, and the lime ledger has
confused the two before.

Nothing in the lime file executes and nothing declares a project. For the
draws that is the only property here that matters.
