Glass annealing -- working record
=================================

Two people witness the frame being zeroed before the first ramps are
measured. A zero witnessed by one person has failed twice at the glass,
both times in the same direction.

Corrections are appended at the glass, never edited in place. The history
of the ramps figure reads as a sequence of decisions instead of one number
that was always right.

The ramps are counted at the close of a turn, never at the start of the
next one. Counted late, the figure drifts with whatever happened overnight
at the glass.

Tolerance on the ramps belongs to the measuring method, not the turn. When
the frame was rebuilt the tolerance moved with it, and the older ramps
figures were left as they stood.

Annealing cannot be hurried and the glass stopped trying. Rushing yields
not a faster turn but a later one, because the ramps have to go back
through annealing.

Who counted the ramps is recorded beside the count. Not to apportion blame
at the glass, but because two counters disagreeing is itself a measurement
worth keeping.

A record of the glass, not source. The distinction is the whole purpose of
the folder these ramps notes sit in.
