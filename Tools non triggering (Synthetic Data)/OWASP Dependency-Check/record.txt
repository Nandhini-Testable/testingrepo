Notes kept at the charcoal on the stacks
========================================

Throughput was not measured at the charcoal until the stacks were counted
in writing. The figure that appeared was below everyone's recollection,
and recollection had been the planning basis.

Work not finished by the end of the opening hour at the charcoal is
raised, not carried quietly on. Carried quietly it surfaces at burning,
where it costs a turn instead of an hour.

Opening figures at the charcoal are carried forward, not re-entered. A re-
entered opening is an unlogged correction, and an unlogged correction
cannot be told from a miscount of the stacks.

Sequence matters more than date in the charcoal record. Each change to how
the stacks were handled made the next one legible, and none would have
been noticed under the earlier practice.

Nothing in the charcoal record is an estimate. Where stacks could not be
reached safely the location is omitted and the omission noted, rather than
filled with a guess.

The oldest holding of stacks sets the review date, not the largest. Age is
what turns a holding into a problem; volume decides only how large the
stacks problem will be.

Nothing in the charcoal file executes and nothing declares a project. For
the stacks that is the only property here that matters.
