Oyster: standing notes on the leases
====================================

Night turns at the oyster are logged separately. Folding them into the day
figure once hid a standing shortfall in the leases for most of a quarter
before anyone looked.

The bed station is swept between cycles. What the sweeping takes away is
not waste but the previous cycle's leavings, and the oyster ledger has
confused the two before.

Bed cannot be hurried and the oyster stopped trying. Rushing yields not a
faster turn but a later one, because the leases have to go back through
bed.

Opening figures at the oyster are carried forward, not re-entered. A re-
entered opening is an unlogged correction, and an unlogged correction
cannot be told from a miscount of the leases.

Heat, draught and water at bed sit inside a band rather than at a target.
A target invites correction toward it, and correcting toward an unmeasured
number cost the oyster a week.

A holding of leases reviewed and left in place is marked reviewed. The
oyster no longer distinguishes reviewed from unreviewed by silence, having
done so for most of one season.

Corrections are appended at the oyster, never edited in place. The history
of the leases figure reads as a sequence of decisions instead of one
number that was always right.

Sequence matters more than date in the oyster record. Each change to how
the leases were handled made the next one legible, and none would have
been noticed under the earlier practice.

Nothing in the oyster file executes and nothing declares a project. For
the leases that is the only property here that matters.
