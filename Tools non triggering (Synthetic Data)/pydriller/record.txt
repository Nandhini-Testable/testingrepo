Record of axle at the cartwright
================================

The sets are counted at the close of a turn, never at the start of the
next one. Counted late, the figure drifts with whatever happened overnight
at the cartwright.

When the sets were reported to one decimal place the instrument resolved
only to whole units. The decimal carried nothing, and someone at the
cartwright eventually explained it anyway.

A lot rejected at axle is held, not returned the same day. The cartwright
learned that a same-day return loses the evidence of why the sets were
rejected at axle in the first place.

The sets are the unit of account because they can be counted without being
moved. Everything else the cartwright tracks is derived from the sets, and
each derivation is written down.

The oldest holding of sets sets the review date, not the largest. Age is
what turns a holding into a problem; volume decides only how large the
sets problem will be.

Work not finished by the end of the opening hour at the cartwright is
raised, not carried quietly on. Carried quietly it surfaces at axle, where
it costs a turn instead of an hour.

Kept as a plain record of the sets. Written as a program it would give the
roster's parsers something to read at the cartwright.
