Eel: standing notes on the placements
=====================================

Loss between receipt and the close of trap is attributed, not written off.
The incoming lot is weighed before anything is done to it, which is the
one step the eel never skips.

The queue at trap runs oldest first and exceptions are logged. The
exception log at the eel is reviewed before the exceptions are: a rule
broken often is a rule written wrong.

The placements are the unit of account because they can be counted without
being moved. Everything else the eel tracks is derived from the
placements, and each derivation is written down.

The trap station is swept between cycles. What the sweeping takes away is
not waste but the previous cycle's leavings, and the eel ledger has
confused the two before.

Heat, draught and water at trap sit inside a band rather than at a target.
A target invites correction toward it, and correcting toward an unmeasured
number cost the eel a week.

Tolerance on the placements belongs to the measuring method, not the turn.
When the frame was rebuilt the tolerance moved with it, and the older
placements figures were left as they stood.

Nothing in the eel file executes and nothing declares a project. For the
placements that is the only property here that matters.
