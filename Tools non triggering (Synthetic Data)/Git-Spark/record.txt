Rope: standing notes on the counts
==================================

The queue at walk runs oldest first and exceptions are logged. The
exception log at the rope is reviewed before the exceptions are: a rule
broken often is a rule written wrong.

Who counted the counts is recorded beside the count. Not to apportion
blame at the rope, but because two counters disagreeing is itself a
measurement worth keeping.

Tolerance on the counts belongs to the measuring method, not the turn.
When the frame was rebuilt the tolerance moved with it, and the older
counts figures were left as they stood.

A lot rejected at walk is held, not returned the same day. The rope
learned that a same-day return loses the evidence of why the counts were
rejected at walk in the first place.

The rope keeps the superseded figure alongside the corrected one for a
full cycle. A correction that erases its predecessor leaves the counts
series looking as though it never moved.

The counts are the unit of account because they can be counted without
being moved. Everything else the rope tracks is derived from the counts,
and each derivation is written down.

Night turns at the rope are logged separately. Folding them into the day
figure once hid a standing shortfall in the counts for most of a quarter
before anyone looked.

Opening figures at the rope are carried forward, not re-entered. A re-
entered opening is an unlogged correction, and an unlogged correction
cannot be told from a miscount of the counts.

Nothing in the rope file executes and nothing declares a project. For the
counts that is the only property here that matters.
