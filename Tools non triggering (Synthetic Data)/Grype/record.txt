Net mending: method and standing practice
=========================================

A lot rejected at mending is held, not returned the same day. The net
learned that a same-day return loses the evidence of why the gauges were
rejected at mending in the first place.

Opening figures at the net are carried forward, not re-entered. A re-
entered opening is an unlogged correction, and an unlogged correction
cannot be told from a miscount of the gauges.

The net keeps the superseded figure alongside the corrected one for a full
cycle. A correction that erases its predecessor leaves the gauges series
looking as though it never moved.

Counting moved to the end of the cycle after the gauges figure was
disputed twice in a month. The dispute ended; the disagreement about what
the net was for did not.

The mending station is swept between cycles. What the sweeping takes away
is not waste but the previous cycle's leavings, and the net ledger has
confused the two before.

A difference between the written figure and the physical count is carried
as an open line, never absorbed. The net has closed lines three turns
after they were raised, and the gauges were right.

Two people witness the frame being zeroed before the first gauges are
measured. A zero witnessed by one person has failed twice at the net, both
times in the same direction.

Nothing in the net file executes and nothing declares a project. For the
gauges that is the only property here that matters.
